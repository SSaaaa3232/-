**Gorden Sun** @Gorden\_Sun 2026-04-13

AI First Organizational Practices

Sharing the experience of AI reconstruction in a 25-person company, written by this company's CTP—it's practically a "copy the homework" level case study.

Previously, it was AI assisting individuals, with people collaborating through processes, yielding only 10%-20% efficiency gains; AI First, however, restructures the entire architecture to be AI-led, where any human-led function reduces overall efficiency and must be replaced by AI. Human roles transform into architects (designing SOPs for AI) and operators (researching, verifying, approving).

Being AI-led requires Harness Engineering transformation to allow AI to see more systems and repositories; designing AI operation processes and loops to automatically assign tasks, execute tasks, and fix issues.  
人工智能优先的组织实践

分享一家 25 人公司中人工智能重建的经验，由该公司的 CTP 撰写——这几乎是一个“抄写作业”级别的案例研究。

过去，是 AI 协助个人，人们通过流程协作，效率提升仅 10%-20%;然而，AI First 将整个架构重构为 AI 主导，任何由人类主导的功能都会降低整体效率，必须被 AI 取代。人类的角色转变为架构师（设计人工智能标准操作程序）和操作员（研究、验证、批准）。

以人工智能为主导需要通过机箱工程转型，使人工智能能够看到更多的系统和存储库;设计 AI 操作流程和循环，自动分配任务、执行任务并解决问题。

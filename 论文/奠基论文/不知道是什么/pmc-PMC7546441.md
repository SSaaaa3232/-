                  - superscript [ref=e116]: +
                  - text: ). These cofactors are expensive to use in vitro and difficult to control in vivo. We demonstrate the development of a noncanonical redox cofactor system based on nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN
                  - superscript [ref=e117]: +
                  - text: ). The key enzyme in the system is a computationally designed glucose dehydrogenase with a 10
                  - superscript [ref=e118]: "7"

                  - text: over NADP

                  - emphasis [ref=e121]: Escherichia coli

                - paragraph [ref=e845]:
                  - text: In summary, we have developed a noncanonical redox cofactor system based on NMN
                  - superscript [ref=e846]: +
                  - text: . We explored its applications in redox cycling in vitro and specific electron delivery in vivo.                   - superscript [ref=e847]: +
                  - text: is the desired cofactor. Our future work will aim to mine a diverse ensemble of oxidoreductase scaffolds

                  - text: ", which could potentially result in a large panel of enzymes capable of harboring NMN"

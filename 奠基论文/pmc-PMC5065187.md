                  - heading "Results" [level=3] [ref=e106]
                  - paragraph [ref=e107]: Metabolites with increasing oxidation potentials (GSSG, CySS, Acoc) increased adipocyte ROS. In contrast, P caused a decrease in ROS compared with L.                 - generic [ref=e108]:
                  - heading "Conclusions" [level=3] [ref=e109]
                  - paragraph [ref=e110]: We demonstrated that in human adipocytes changes in the external redox state impacted ROS production, LPO, energy efficiency, lipid handling, and differentiation. A more oxidized state generally led to increased ROS, LPO and lipid turnover and more reduction led to increased respiration and a proton leak. However, not all of the redox couples were the same suggesting compartmentalization. These data are consistent with the concept of the circulating redox metabolome as a master metabolic regulatory system.

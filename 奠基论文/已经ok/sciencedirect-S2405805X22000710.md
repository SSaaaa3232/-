## 摘要

Microbial production of value-added products is a promising alternative to plant- and chemical-based routes. However, only few of interested chemicals are producing at bulk scale with microbial cell factories while most are staying at bench level. To enable the microbes toward efficient bioproduction, extensive endeavors have been expended on enzyme exploration and pathway decoration, leaving the role of cofactors, especially the rare cofactors like SAM, FMN(H2) and FAD(H2), largely unconsidered.

本文讨论了通过操纵 NADPH、FAD(H2) 和 SAM 等辅因子来驱动酵母中酚酸（咖啡酸和阿魏酸）的生物合成的研究。

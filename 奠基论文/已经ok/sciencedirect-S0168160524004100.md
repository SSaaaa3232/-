## 摘要

Redox metabolism is pivotal in anaerobic fermentative processes such as winemaking where it results in the production of many metabolites that contribute to the aroma and flavour of wine. Key to this system are NAD^+ and NADP^+, which play essential roles as cofactors in maintaining cellular redox balance and regulating metabolism during fermentation. This review comprehensively explores redox metabolism under winemaking conditions, highlighting the influence of factors such as oxygen availability and vitamins including B3 and B1.

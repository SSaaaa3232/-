                - paragraph [ref=e704]: The principle of pathway assay is to measure the pathway activity by the product of the combined reactions of multiple enzymes. By comparing the pathway activities in the following experiments, one can deduce the possible limiting steps.
                - list [ref=e705]:
                  - listitem [ref=e706]:
                    - generic [ref=e707]: (1)
                    - paragraph [ref=e708]: Reaction mixture (or cell extract) without supplementing with any enzymes. This serves as a baseline measurement to show that the reaction mixture can perform the desired reaction sequence.
                  - listitem [ref=e709]:
                    - generic [ref=e710]: (2)
                    - paragraph [ref=e711]: Reaction mixture (or cell extract) supplemented with all enzymes to a given level. This serves as a positive control to show that the pathway activity can be elevated by the addition of all enzymes together.
                  - listitem [ref=e712]:
                    - generic [ref=e713]: (3)
                    - paragraph [ref=e714]: Reaction mixture (or cell extract) supplemented with all but one enzyme to a given level.
